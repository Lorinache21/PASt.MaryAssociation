Who's who

Committee roles

Our committee is elected by parents and guardians for a two-year term. Names will be published once confirmed — email us if you'd like to know who to contact, or to put your name forward at the next AGM.

  • ChairpersonChairs meetings, liaises with the Principal
  • SecretaryMinutes, agendas and circulation
  • TreasurerAccounts and fundraising records
  • Public Relations OfficerWebsite, social media and email
  • Vice positionsDeputise for each officer
  • NPC Primary representativeOur link to the National Parents Council

Parent nominees on the Board of Management are automatically members of the PA committee.
