Contact

Say hello

Email is the only way to reach us — there are no forms on this website, and we don't use a phone line or social media accounts.

Email the PA

One address, one inbox, read by whoever is on committee duty that week. We're volunteers, so give us a few days to reply.

[contact email]

Opening this link will start an email in your own email app. Nothing is sent until you press send, and this website never sees your message.

Not the school office

For anything about enrolment, absences, uniforms, teaching or school policy, please contact St. Mary's directly. The PA can't answer those questions and can't pass messages on to teachers.
