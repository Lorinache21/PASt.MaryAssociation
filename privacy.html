Privacy notice

Your privacy

No cookies, no tracking, no forms.

Who we are

This website is run by the Parents' Association of St. Mary's Primary School, Johnstown Road, Enfield, Co. Meath, A83 X316. The association is a voluntary group of parents and guardians. You can reach us at Stmaryspaenfield@gmail.com.

This is the PA's own website, not the school's official website.

What this website does not do

  • No cookies. We don't set any cookies.
  • No analytics or tracking. No Google Analytics, pixels, advertising tags or session recording.
  • No forms. Contact happens through your own email app or Facebook.
  • No embedded content. No maps, videos, social feeds or fonts loaded from other companies. Every file this site needs comes from this site.
  • No personal data collected through the website itself.
  • Almost nothing stored on your device. The only exception: if you use the Accessibility menu, your choices (for example dark mode or larger text) are remembered in your browser so they apply on every page. They stay on your device, hold no personal information and are never sent to us. “Reset all settings” clears them.

Server logs

Like any website, the service that hosts these pages may keep basic technical logs (such as the page requested and the time). We don't use them to identify anyone. There are no accounts or passwords on this site.

Photos

We only publish photographs where we have the permission of the parent or guardian of any child who can be identified. We never publish children's names, class names or anything else that would identify a child. If a photo of your child appears here and you'd like it removed, email us and we will take it down promptly.

Email and Facebook

If you email us or message the PA on Facebook, we use your details only to reply and to organise PA activities. Facebook is run by a separate company with its own privacy practices. If you'd like your correspondence deleted, just ask.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to other sites, such as gov.ie, Google Maps, Facebook or the National Parents Council Primary. Once you follow a link, their privacy practices apply, not ours.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal data through this website, there is generally nothing to show, correct or delete. You have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission:

  • Website: dataprotection.ie
  • Phone: 01 765 0100 / 1800 437 737
  • Post: Data Protection Commission, 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28

We'd always rather you came to us first, so we can put things right.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes we'll update this page. Last updated: November 2026.
