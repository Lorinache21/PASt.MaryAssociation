Privacy

Privacy notice

Short, because there isn't much to say. This website doesn't collect information about you.

Who we are

This website is run by the Parents' Association of St. Mary's Primary School, Johnstown Road, Enfield, Co. Meath, A83 X316. The association is a voluntary group of parents and guardians. You can reach us at [contact email].

We are not the school. This is an independent volunteer website, not the school's official website.

What this website does not do

  • No cookies. We don't set any cookies, first-party or otherwise.
  • No analytics. We don't use Google Analytics, Statcounter, or any other measurement tool.
  • No tracking. No pixels, no advertising tags, no session recording, no fingerprinting.
  • No forms. There is nothing to fill in and nothing to submit. Contact happens through your own email app.
  • No embedded content. No maps, videos, social feeds or fonts loaded from other companies. Every file this site needs comes from this site.
  • No personal data collected. We don't ask for your name, your email address, or anything about your child.

The one thing saved on your device

If you use the light/dark switch, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device so the site remembers it next time. That's a single preference value (light or dark). It stays on your device, it is never sent to us or anyone else, and clearing your browser data removes it. If you never touch the switch, nothing is saved.

Server logs

Like any website, the service that hosts these pages may keep basic technical logs (such as the page requested and the time). We don't use them to identify anyone, and we don't combine them with anything else. We don't have a login system, so there are no accounts and no passwords on this site.

Photos

We only publish photographs where we have the permission of the parent or guardian of any child who can be identified. We never publish children's names, class names or anything else that would identify a child. If a photo of your child appears on this site and you would like it removed, email us and we will take it down promptly, without needing a reason.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to other websites, such as the school's own site, gov.ie, the Meath Chronicle or the National Parents Council Primary. Once you follow a link you are on that website, and their privacy practices apply, not ours. We don't track which links you click.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal data through this website, there is generally nothing for us to show you, correct or delete. If you have emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, just ask and we'll remove it.

You have the right to complain to the Irish supervisory authority, the Data Protection Commission:

  • Website: dataprotection.ie
  • Phone: 01 765 0100 / 1800 437 737
  • Post: Data Protection Commission, 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28

We'd always rather you came to us first, so we can put things right.
