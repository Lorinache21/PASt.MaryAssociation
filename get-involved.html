Get involved

There's room for you

Every parent and guardian is a member. Here's how to give a little, or a lot.

Three routes in

Pick whichever fits

If you're a parent or guardian of a child at St. Mary's, you're already a member of the PA — no sign-up needed.

One-off help

An hour at an event. Twenty minutes setting out chairs. One Saturday morning. This is the easiest way in and honestly the most useful.

Four officer roles — Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer and Public Relations Officer — plus vice positions and co-opted helpers. Elections are held at the AGM.

Joining the committee

How committee elections work

  • The committee is elected by the members for a two-year term.
  • Put yourself forward in writing before the AGM, or in person on the night.
  • If more than one person is nominated for a role, there's a secret ballot of members at the AGM.
  • Previous committee members can stand again; no one holds the same officer role for consecutive terms, so there's always room for new faces.
  • All committee members complete Garda Vetting through the school after election.
Read the full constitution

No pressure, no guilt

  • We won't chase you if you can't make something.
  • We won't publish a list of who did and didn't help.
  • We won't ask anyone to spend their own money.
  • We will say thank you, properly and often.

Just get in touch

There are no forms on this website. Tell us a little about what you'd like to do — or simply say hello — and we'll take it from there.
