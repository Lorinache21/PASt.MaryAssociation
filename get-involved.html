Get involved

Help in whatever way suits you

You don't have to join anything, and you don't have to commit to a rota. There are as many ways to help as there are families in the school.

Three routes in

Pick whichever fits

One-off help

An hour at a cake sale. Twenty minutes setting out chairs. A tray of buns. One Saturday morning at the fair.

This is the easiest way in, and honestly the most useful. Most of what we do is made of small, one-off bits of help.

Email us with "one-off" in the subject →

Join the committee

A handful of meetings a year, and a share of the organising. We're rebuilding at the moment, so there's real scope to decide what the PA does next.

Roles include chairperson, secretary, treasurer and event leads — but you don't need any of those titles to be useful.

Ask about the committee →

Donate your time or skills

Good with a spreadsheet? Handy with a drill? Can you take a decent photo, design a poster, or talk to local shops about raffle prizes?

Skills are often more valuable to us than money — and they're a lot more fun to give.

What it's actually like

No pressure, no guilt

Some parents worry that putting their hand up means signing away every free evening for a year. It doesn't. We ask for specific things, with specific time commitments, and "not this time" is always a perfectly good answer.

  • We won't chase you if you can't make something.
  • We won't publish a list of who did and didn't help.
  • We won't ask anyone to spend their own money.
  • We will say thank you, properly and often.

Small things that genuinely help

  • An hourSetting up, clearing away, or standing at a gate.
  • Something bakedBuns, brack, biscuits — shop-bought is fine too.
  • A skillPosters, photos, accounts, carpentry, local contacts.
  • A good ideaHonestly. Some of our best events started as a passing comment.

Just send an email

There are no forms on this website and nothing to sign up to. Tell us a little about what you'd like to do — or just say hello — and we'll take it from there.
