What the PA does

Four things, done properly

Raise funds for the school, run good events, welcome new families, and give parents a friendly way to be heard.

Fundraising

We raise funds for the school and for school projects — always with the prior agreement of the Board of Management. When we raise money for something, we tell you what it's for and what it paid for.

Our Christmas cards and keepsakes fundraiser is the first of the year.

Community events

We draw up a plan of activities with the parent body and the Principal: events that promote the involvement of parents in the life of the school and give families a reason to meet.

Welcoming new families

Starting school is a big deal for the child and for whoever is doing the drop-off. Our Chairperson represents the PA in meetings with new parents, and we try to be a friendly face from day one.

A voice for parents

We advise the Principal and the Board of Management on matters relating to the school, and we keep parents informed by publishing meeting agendas and minutes.

Working in partnership

How we work with the school

Because the Parents' Association operates solely for the school, its activities are the responsibility of the school's Board of Management (BOM). So we work closely with them:

  • We meet the BOM at the start of each school year to review our proposed programme of activities.
  • We meet the Principal once a term (or more often if needed).
  • We publish our agendas and minutes to all parents and report at the AGM.
  • We affiliate each year to the National Parents Council Primary.

How we try to work

  • Keep it light. No guilt, no pressure.
  • Say what the money is for. Before we ask, not after.
  • Include everyone. Every family, every budget.
  • Keep confidences. Trust matters; confidentiality is essential.

Fundraising & finances

Good financial governance

We follow the Financial Support Services Unit (FSSU) guideline "Good Financial Governance and the Parents' Association" (Financial Guideline P10), together with our own constitution.

BOM approval first

Before any fundraising, we consult the Board of Management and get its approval. Good practice is to record the agreement: the purpose, how the funds will be raised, when they'll be transferred to the BOM and the expected timeframe.

Only what we need

The PA bank account holds only the funds needed for day-to-day running costs. Anything beyond that is transferred to the main school bank account as soon as practicable.

Open books

The PA account is included in the school's annual accounts approved by the Board. Our Treasurer keeps a separate record of every fundraising transaction and presents accounts at each year's AGM.

What we don't do

The PA does not run the school or set school policy. We don't hire staff, decide admissions or manage the curriculum.

It is not the role of the PA or its officers to pursue a complaint on behalf of an individual or group of parents. If you have a concern, we'll gladly point you to the school's complaints procedure. For anything about teaching, enrolment, uniforms or absences, please contact the school office.
