What the PA does

Four things, done properly

The Parents' Association exists to raise a bit of money, run good events, welcome new families, and give parents a friendly way to be heard. That's it — and it's plenty.

Fundraising

Cake sales, the Christmas fair, sponsored walks, a raffle or two. We try to keep fundraising light on families: nobody should feel they have to spend money to be part of the school.

When we raise money for something specific, we say so — and we tell you afterwards what it paid for.

Community events

Movie nights, coffee mornings after drop-off, a summer get-together, and the small excuses that let parents actually talk to each other.

Events are open to the whole school community, including grandparents and younger siblings.

Welcoming new families

Starting school is a big deal — for the child and for whoever is doing the drop-off. We try to make the first term easier with a welcome coffee, a friendly face at the gate, and someone to ask the small questions.

A voice for parents

If a number of families raise the same question, it helps to have one place to gather it. We can pass on themes and suggestions to the school, and we can share information back out.

We are a channel, not a decision-maker.

What we don't do

The PA does not run the school and does not set school policy. We don't hire staff, decide on admissions, manage the curriculum, or speak for the school. We aren't the Board of Management either.

If your question is about teaching, enrolment, uniforms, behaviour, absences or anything else about how the school runs, please contact the school office directly. If it's about an event, a fundraiser or joining the PA, we're your people.

Where we're at

Getting going again

The association has been quiet for a while, and we're in the middle of getting it going again — new energy, a few familiar faces, and a short list of things we'd like to do this year.

That makes this a genuinely good moment to get involved. There's no long-standing way of doing things to work around, and plenty of room to shape what the PA becomes.

How we try to work

  • Keep it light. No guilt, no pressure, no long meetings.
  • Say what the money is for. Before we ask, not after.
  • Include everyone. Every family, every budget, every amount of free time.
  • Stay in our lane. Events and fundraising, not school policy.

This is the PA's first website. If something here is unclear, tell us and we'll fix it.
