Our crest

Ár nDícheall — "our best effort"

The school crest tells the story of St. Mary's: sport, music, art and books around a circle of joined hands; a tree, a church, a river and a country road leading on. Our motto, Ár nDícheall, means "our best effort" — something every child, teacher and parent in the school community can share.

The Parents' Association crest adds the figures of families gathering around the shield, and the ribbon that says who we are: St. Mary's Parents' Association.

Who to ask about what

Questions about teaching, enrolment, uniforms, absences or school policy belong with the school office. The PA looks after fundraising, events and the parent voice.

The place we call home

Enfield, Co. Meath

Enfield is a village in County Meath, on the Royal Canal. For many of our families it is home — and the school gate is where neighbours meet. St. Mary's is at the centre of it all.

Our events are for the whole school community: parents, guardians, grandparents, and the younger brothers and sisters still waiting for their turn at St. Mary's.
