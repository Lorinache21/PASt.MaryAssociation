Ethos, in plain words

St. Mary's is a Catholic primary school, and its ethos shapes the day-to-day life of the school — the prayers, the seasons, the way children are encouraged to look out for one another.

As a Parents' Association we are not a religious body and we don't speak for the school on matters of ethos. We simply try to support the school community as it is, and to make sure every family feels welcome in it.

Who to ask about what

Questions about teaching, enrolment, uniforms, absences or school policy belong with the school office, not with the PA. The PA looks after fundraising and community events.
