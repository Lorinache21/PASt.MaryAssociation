On now

Christmas at St. Mary's

On now · Christmas

Personalised Christmas cards & keepsake gifts

Our first event as a newly established Parents' Association. Order personalised Christmas cards and a range of memorable keepsake gifts featuring your child's very own artwork, through our online provider. The children have worked incredibly hard on their Christmas drawings this week, which makes these gifts extra special for family and friends.

  1. Look out for the letter in your child's schoolbag with full ordering details and a QR code.
  2. Scan the QR code and choose your cards and keepsakes online.
  3. Order early — our provider encourages early ordering to ensure delivery in time for Christmas.

All money raised goes towards our General School Funds for the year ahead, supporting activities, resources and initiatives for all the children in St. Mary's.

Fundraising is carried out with the agreement of the school's Board of Management. Thank you for your continued support.

The year ahead

We're newly established, so our calendar is just getting started. Dates will be posted here and on Facebook as soon as they're agreed with the school.

Every school year

Annual General Meeting

Held early in the school year, before the end of October. At least two weeks' written notice of the agenda goes to all parents. The Treasurer presents the accounts for the previous school year and we hear your views on new ideas.

Open to all

Open PA meetings

A minimum of three meetings each year are open to every parent and guardian. Agendas go out at least one week before, and minutes within a week afterwards.

There's nothing to sign up for on this website. To help at an event or check whether something is going ahead, email Stmaryspaenfield@gmail.com or message us on Facebook (Stmaryspa Enfield).

How general meetings work

Meetings & notice

  • 14 days' notice is given to parents ahead of all general meetings.
  • Meetings are held during term time so as many parents as possible can attend.
  • An Extraordinary General Meeting can be called by the committee, or on a written request signed by at least 40 parents.
  • Votes at an AGM are by secret ballot for contested roles.
