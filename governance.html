Governance

Our constitution & finances

How the Parents' Association is run, who does what, and how we look after the money.

Our aim

A forum for parents, in partnership with the school

To provide a forum for the parents and guardians of children attending St. Mary's PS, Enfield, to work together in partnership with the Principal, Staff, Board of Management and the whole school community, to help provide the best possible education for the children in the school.

This page summarises our constitution and the financial guidance we follow. The full documents are available to download.

PA Constitution (PDF)FSSU Guideline P10 (PDF)

Our role under the Education Act 1998

A parents' association shall promote the interests of the students in a school in co-operation with the board, principal, teachers and students. Two broad tasks:

  • To advise the Principal or the BOM on matters relating to the school.
  • To adopt a programme of activities that promotes parents' involvement in the school, in consultation with the Principal.

Membership & committee

Who's who in the PA

All parents and guardians of pupils of St. Mary's are members. Members elect a committee for a two-year term. Four officer positions must be filled to form a committee; vice positions are also sought (vice-treasurer, vice-chairperson, vice-secretary, vice-PRO, in that order).

Chairperson

  • Chairs meetings and liaises with the Principal
  • Represents the PA in meetings with new parents
  • Co-ordinates the AGM
  • Sets the agenda after consulting the Principal and BOM
  • Ensures minutes are agreed and circulated
  • Forwards matters outside the PA's remit to the right person or group
  • Can convene special meetings on issues of serious significance

Secretary

  • Minutes each meeting and the AGM
  • Attends meetings with the Principal alongside the Chairperson
  • Circulates the agenda at least one week before each full PA meeting
  • Forwards the minutes to the PA within a week of the meeting

Treasurer

  • Keeps the accounts of the PA
  • Gives each meeting a summary of the accounts
  • Details fundraising proceeds and how funds are spent
  • Presents accounts to the AGM for the previous school year
  • Liaises with the bank or other financial institutions

Public Relations Officer

  • Promotes the PA's activities in the media
  • Promotes the PA on social media and the school website
  • Monitors social media and email correspondence, and handles updates at the committee's direction

Good to know

Vice positions deputise for the relevant officer. Parent nominees elected to the Board of Management are automatically members of the PA committee but don't hold officer positions. At the end of their term, officers complete a thorough handover with their successors.

The detail

How the PA runs

Elections & terms
  • An AGM is held at the start of each academic year — before the end of October.
  • The committee serves a two-year term; at every second AGM all committee members step down and may stand again.
  • Nominations are made in writing before the AGM or in person at it. Contested roles are decided by secret ballot of members present.
  • No member holds the same officer position for consecutive terms; a balance of new and experienced members is preferred.
  • All committee members complete Garda Vetting through the school after election.
  • Officer vacancies during a term are filled from existing committee members who don't hold an officer role.
Committee meetings & decisions
  • The committee meets regularly; at least three meetings a year are open to all members.
  • At least three committee members are needed to take a decision; a simple majority carries it, with the Chairperson holding a casting vote.
  • The committee may co-opt helpers and set up sub-committees, which are accountable to the main committee.
  • A member absent from three consecutive meetings without notice is deemed to have resigned. Resignations go to the Chairperson in writing.
Working with the school
  • The PA meets the BOM at the start of each year to review its programme of activities.
  • The Chairperson and Secretary meet the Principal once a term (or any two officers if they can't attend).
  • The PA reports at the AGM and publishes agendas and minutes to all parents.
  • It is not the PA's role to pursue complaints for individuals or groups; concerns are referred to the school's complaints procedure.
General meetings
  • AGM: at least two weeks' written notice of the agenda to all parents; includes the Treasurer's statement of accounts for the previous school year, a progress report and a chance to canvass parents' views on new initiatives.
  • EGM: called by a committee majority, or on a written request signed by at least 40 parents. The Secretary must summon it within one month. Held in term time with 14 days' notice.
National Parents Council Primary & confidentiality
  • The PA affiliates to the National Parents Council Primary every year, with a committee member as authorised representative.
  • The PA represents parents' views, and confidentiality is essential — a breach of trust would compromise the association.
Amending the constitution
  • The constitution is reviewed every two years when a new committee is elected.
  • Amendments need a simple majority of those at an AGM or an EGM called for the purpose, with written notice to the Secretary at least two weeks beforehand.
  • The constitution was originally passed at an EGM of St. Mary's PA on 3 June 2015.

Money matters

Fundraising & finances

Fundraising for the school is done only with the prior agreement of the BOM. The PA gives its agreed opinion to the BOM on projects for which funds are to be raised, and any spending of PA funds needs the BOM's prior approval.

We follow FSSU Financial Guideline P10:

  • Consult the BOM and get approval before fundraising; record the purpose, method, transfer timeline and expected project timeframe.
  • Keep only day-to-day running funds in the PA account; transfer the rest to the school's main account as soon as practicable.
  • Include the PA account in the school's annual accounts approved by the Board, with a separate record of all fundraising transactions.

Source: Financial Support Services Unit, Financial Guideline P10 (2017/2018). The FSSU can be reached at primary@fssu.ie or 01 910 4020.
