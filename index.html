The Parents' Association of St. Mary's Primary School is a forum for every parent and guardian to work in partnership with the Principal, staff and Board of Management — to help provide the best possible education for the children of our school.

On now · Christmas

Personalised Christmas cards & keepsake gifts

Our first event as a newly established Parents' Association. Order personalised Christmas cards and a range of memorable keepsake gifts featuring your child's very own artwork, through our online provider. The children have worked incredibly hard on their Christmas drawings this week, which makes these gifts extra special for family and friends.

  1. Look out for the letter in your child's schoolbag with full ordering details and a QR code.
  2. Scan the QR code and choose your cards and keepsakes online.
  3. Order early — our provider encourages early ordering to ensure delivery in time for Christmas.

All money raised goes towards our General School Funds for the year ahead, supporting activities, resources and initiatives for all the children in St. Mary's.

A warm hello

Every family has something to give

We are a newly established committee of parents and guardians, and we'd love you to be part of what comes next.

Some people help at one event a year. Some bake. Some share a skill or a good idea. Some join the committee and help steer the whole thing. All of it counts, and none of it is taken for granted.

Under the Education Act 1998, a parents' association promotes the interests of the students in a school in co-operation with the board, principal, teachers and students — and that's exactly what we're here to do.
