A warm hello

Every family has something to give

The Parents' Association is made up of mammies, daddies, guardians and grandparents from across the school. We meet a handful of times a year, and we do our best to keep things light, useful and genuinely enjoyable.

Some people help at one event a year. Some bake. Some stand at a gate with a bucket and a smile. Some join the committee and help steer the whole thing. All of it counts, and none of it is taken for granted.

This is our first website, and we're still filling in the corners — so if something is missing or out of date, please tell us.
